About Mr. Khan

The story behind Karachi's home for bold, handcrafted Afghani flavour.

More Than Just Fast Food

Mr. Khan Burger House started with a simple idea: bring the bold, smoky flavour of Afghani street food to Karachi's fast-food scene — without cutting corners on quality. What began as a small setup in Federal B Area has grown into a name locals trust for a fast, filling, flavour-packed meal.

Our mission is simple — bigger portions, bolder spice, and better ingredients in every single order. We grill our meat fresh, mix our own spice blends in-house, and prepare every wrap, burger, and kabab to order. Never in advance.

From our signature Phuppo chutni to our loaded Zinger Ultra, everything on our menu is built around one goal: giving you a bite worth coming back for.
